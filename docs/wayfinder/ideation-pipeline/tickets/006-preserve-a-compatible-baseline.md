## Question

How much ideation behavior may change before a trustworthy baseline exists?

## Resolution

First preserve the original action, reflection, finalization, and idea-schema behavior while changing only required boundaries, model access, input preparation, and observability. Later optimizations must be versioned and justified by evidence.
