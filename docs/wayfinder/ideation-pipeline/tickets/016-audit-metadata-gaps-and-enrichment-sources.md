## Question

Which Target Paper and reference fields are missing, malformed, or insufficient for Workshop Files and local retrieval, and which authoritative public sources can fill them with reproducible provenance?

## Resolution

[Deterministic audit and source research](../../../research/metadata-gaps-and-enrichment-sources.md) confirms complete, anomaly-free target/reference membership; 18 missing venues are recoverable by exact DOI, and three reference abstracts are unusable, with two recoverable and one authoritatively unavailable. Every paper has an exact DOI, PMID, arXiv ID, or DBLP key, so no fuzzy/global search is required. Citation completeness, field provenance, abstract rights, and the unavailable-abstract policy remain design decisions.
