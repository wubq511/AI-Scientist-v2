## Question

When may an observed problem lead to a system-design change?

## Resolution

Follow the comparative-selection rule in `AGENTS.md`: never promote a technical approach from intuition alone. Record the problem, falsifiable hypotheses, viable alternatives, comparison metrics, failure cases, before/after evidence, and regressions. Prefer one major variable at a time. Choose observed effectiveness first, then the simplest and lightest option among those meeting the evidence threshold.
